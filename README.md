# Touchline 8 — Football Manager

**Beta · 0.12.0 · build 14**

Build your club from the touchline: choose the team, handle matchday decisions, develop players, negotiate deals and manage the club's finances.

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/TranquilMT/Touchline-Manager/releases/latest)

## What can you do in the game?

### Manage a club
- Start a career with an existing club or create your own club identity.
- Choose from a database of 204 clubs, 5,169 named players and 10 divisions.
- Pick your starting XI and bench manually, or use automatic squad selection.
- Set formations, mentality, pressing and tactical instructions.
- Follow league tables, results, domestic cups and the Champions Cup.
- Track simulated World Cup, European Championship, AFCON and AFC Asian Cup tournaments.

### Take control on matchday
- Watch the moving live pitch with ball carriers, support runs, pressing, passes and interceptions.
- See chances from the goal-facing view with shooting, keeper dives, catches, parries and net movement.
- Aim penalties and free kicks by tapping or dragging on the goal.
- Choose power and technique, and touch the ball to control curl and lift.
- Set your goalkeeper direction and wall instructions when defending a set piece.
- Pause, change match speed, make substitutions or simulate to full time.
- Deliver half-time team talks and adjust your approach.
- Review goal chances, misses, saves, woodwork and positional VAR/offside checks.
- Follow commentary, momentum, match statistics and fan/bench reactions.

### Build and develop your squad
- Scout and compare players, then negotiate transfer fees, wages and contracts.
- Sell players and manage squad depth, fitness, injuries and suspensions.
- Review player attributes, Football IQ, morale, form and potential.
- Run targeted training for player development and short-term boosts.
- Bring in academy youngsters, develop them, promote them or release them.

### Run the club
- View detailed stadium illustrations for 30 major clubs across 28 venues, with four shared ground types for other clubs.
- Explore stadium artwork with zoom and stand names placed on architectural fascias.
- Lease food kiosks, a supporters’ bar and a stadium restaurant; collect home-match rent and sales shares.
- Open VIP access, set premium prices and track catering costs and income.
- Set ticket prices, sell season tickets and forecast attendance.
- Manage budgets, wages, sponsorships and upgrade costs.
- Improve the stadium, training facilities, medical centre and academy.
- Create kits and sign sponsorship placements for kits and stadium assets.

### Handle people and club stories
- Speak with players about form, confidence, roles and standards.
- Negotiate with agents through conversations with changing terms and patience.
- Attend pre-match and post-match press conferences and invite players to speak.
- Respond to fictional player, staff and club incidents.
- Read your inbox, respond to club matters and follow supporter reactions.

### Keep your career
- Save locally using multiple career slots.
- Export and import your career through Settings.
- Continue an existing preview-app career after installing a compatible update.

## Current scope

Match outcomes use a sampled-possession/event simulation; the moving live pitch illustrates play between highlights. The wider feature programme remains in progress. Player attributes and parts of the roster data are game estimates.

Stadium backgrounds are static artistic interpretations of grounds and their surroundings, rather than surveyed replicas. Kenney CC0 turf, footballs, flags and referee cards are integrated into the tactical match view. Existing illustrated close-up goalkeeper and goalpost artwork is preserved.

The current build passes 88 automated tests, production compilation and database validation. Physical Android layout and touch testing remain ongoing. See the release notes and attached checklist for detailed progress and limitations.
